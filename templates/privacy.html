Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly when using Spendly, including your name, email address, and account password (stored in hashed form). We also collect the expense records you create, such as amount, category, date, and description. We do not collect payment card details, bank account numbers, or other financial account information — Spendly only stores the expense entries you choose to log manually.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate and improve the Service for you: to authenticate your account, display your expense history, calculate spending summaries and category breakdowns, and let you filter your records by date. We do not use your personal expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in a database associated with your Spendly account and are retained for as long as your account remains active. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before being saved. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. The Service may rely on limited third-party infrastructure (such as font or hosting providers) required to deliver the application to you. These providers do not have access to your expense records and only process the minimum data necessary to provide their service.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, or if you would like to request that your account and associated data be deleted, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page or via the support channel linked from the app.
